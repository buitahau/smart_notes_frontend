Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 5, 2024

analytics

Information We Collect

We believe in radical transparency. Smart Notes is designed to collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide a high-quality note-taking experience while you browse.

  • Account Information: Email address and username when you create an account.
  • Note Content: The text, highlights, and annotations you explicitly save using the extension.
  • Technical Metadata: Browser type, operating system, and extension version for troubleshooting and performance optimization.
  • Usage Data: Non-identifiable interaction metrics such as features used and frequency of notes taken.

encrypted

Data Storage & Security

Security is not an afterthought; it is our foundation. All data transmitted between the extension and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

We utilize Tier-1 cloud infrastructure providers that maintain rigorous security certifications (SOC2, ISO 27001) to host our databases and application logic. Your notes are stored in an encrypted format at rest.

share

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, or in the event of a merger or acquisition where your data would remain protected under similar terms.

"Your privacy is our priority. We maintain strict internal protocols to ensure your data is never accessed without cause."

rule

Your Choices & Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you can:

  • Access & Export: Download a full archive of all your saved notes and highlights.
  • Edit & Delete: Modify or permanently remove any specific note or your entire account.
  • Communication Preferences: Opt-out of non-essential email communications via your account settings.
extension

Browser Extension Permissions

To function effectively, the Smart Notes extension requires specific browser permissions:

  • Active Tab: To allow you to take notes on the page you are currently viewing.
  • Storage: To save your preferences and local cache for offline access.
  • Context Menus: To add the "Save to Smart Notes" option when you right-click on text.

Questions or Requests?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please don't hesitate to reach out. We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 48 hours.
